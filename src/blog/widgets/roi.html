Overlap raters; never read a raw average

A program whose raters do not overlap cannot separate its raters from its agent, and raw averages misread the agent exactly when the roster changes.

Report families, not an index

The index hides where the agent changed. The families are where engineering acts, and where the change log can be checked against the scores.

Measure your own drift rate

How often to evaluate has a parameter: the movement your records do not account for. An agent with pinned vendor snapshots and version-controlled configuration drifts less.

Design for attribution before you start

Record which finding motivated each change. Vary evaluation intensity on purpose. Afterwards, there is no comparison arm.
