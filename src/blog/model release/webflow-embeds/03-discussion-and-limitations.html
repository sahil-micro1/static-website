7 Discussion and limitations

The 3.44-point HLE gain and improvements on both FrontierScience splits and AA-LCR show higher measured performance after the combined post-training recipe. The practical contribution is to connect expert task design, complete reference solutions, precise grading, and adaptive practice. The selected cases illustrate why feedback must preserve the requested object and verify every required component; observed success and response length guide where to collect further feedback.

This study complements work on reasoning through reinforcement learning [1, 2] and group-relative training [6], extending the task-authoring and review perspective of STEM QA Frontier [3] to post-training outcomes.

Observed success and reward contrast guide task selection, but do not directly measure a task’s contribution to subsequent learning. Next steps are to train on a larger expert dataset spanning more domains and difficulty levels, with controlled comparisons to test how matching tasks to the model’s current learning needs affects improvement. We will also explore partial rewards for verifiable intermediate steps and correctly solved subproblems, testing whether richer feedback improves learning on long, difficult tasks while preserving final-answer correctness and resistant to reward hacking.
