4. Decomposing model performance

The leaderboard already differentiates models at the aggregate level. To understand where this differentiation arises, we further decompose performance along the core axes of our rubric taxonomy (negotiation stage, required action, priority, and evaluation dimension), while also assessing the robustness of these findings along other dimensions of the benchmark design: playbook complexity, agreement type, and jurisdiction. These analyses corroborate one another, yield meaningful separation in model capabilities, and demonstrate that the Ivo-micro1 ContractBench taxonomy serves as a durable methodology for assessing future models.
